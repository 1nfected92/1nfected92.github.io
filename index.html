GOOD TO KNOW

Your questions, answered.

What should I expect during my first visit?

Your session begins with a brief conversation about your goals and comfort preferences. Veronica then tailors the treatment to your needs.

How early should I arrive?

Arrive 10 minutes before your appointment so you can settle in and complete any necessary intake information.

Which treatment is right for me?

Reflexology is a focused foot treatment, Thai Massage emphasizes stretching and compression, Sports Massage supports active bodies, and Full Body Massage is ideal for broad relaxation.

Do I need to be 18 or older?

Yes. The Soleful Goddess welcomes guests age 18 and older.
